# Specifying Length - recording notes

Deck: `clear-instructions-length.pptx` (5 slides), built by
`build_clear_instructions_length.py`.

## Purpose

This lesson teaches learners to replace vague words such as short with a measurable
word range, item count or reading time, plus a content priority.

## Speaking notes

1. **Specifying Length**
   - Length is part of the output contract.
2. **Length is part of the output contract**
   - Compare keep it short with a 120-to-150-word target.
3. **Length works with audience and priority**
   - The limit needs a statement of what must survive the cut.
4. **The answer fits the place where it will be used**
   - Measurable outputs are easier to compare and edit.
5. **Live handoff**
   - Generate the same summary with a vague limit and a measured one.
