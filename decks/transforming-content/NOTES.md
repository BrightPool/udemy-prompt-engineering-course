# Transforming Content for Your Audience - recording notes

Deck: `transforming-content.pptx` (6 slides), built by `build_transforming_content.py`.

**Replaces "Explain It Like I'm Five"** (re-film). ELI5 is now one example of the
technique: reading level turned all the way down.

## Purpose

Teaches transformation as a general technique: keep the facts, change the form so a
different reader can use it. Simpler, more expert, shorter, another format, another
language or another tone.

## Speaking notes

1. **Transforming Content for Your Audience**
   - Same information, the form a different reader needs.
2. **Same facts, a new form for a new reader**
   - One set of meeting notes fans out to an email, a board outline, a plain-language
     explainer and a translation.
3. **How it works - six dials you can turn**
   - Audience, reading level, length, format, language, tone. Point out ELI5 as one setting.
4. **Why it matters - the facts survive, the shape changes**
   - Messy notes become a four-line email. Check the date, owner and figure all survived.
5. **The prompt pattern**
   - Audience, purpose, format, length and tone, and what must stay exactly the same.
   - Limit: simplifying can distort. Ask it to list anything it left out or rounded off.
6. **Let's see it in action**
   - Paste real notes, ask for a team email, reuse the same notes for a board outline,
     check the facts.
